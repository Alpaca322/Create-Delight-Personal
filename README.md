---

## 📖 概述

基于 Create-Delight-Remake(1.20.1)，本项目支持 Minecraft 1.21.1 Neoforge ，融合原有模组更新、用非官方重制填补缺失部分，并去除不被支持的模组，打造流畅而丰富的游戏体验。（最重要的是支持航空学！！！）

---

## ✨ 已完成的工作

### 1. 🚀 大规模模组升级
- 将**绝大多数**模组更新至最新的 1.21.1 兼容版本，带来更好的性能、稳定性和新特性。

### 2. 🧩 新增内容 – 我的最爱
- 加入了 **Aeronautics**（航空学）及相关热门模组，拓展探索与机械创造的维度。
- 精心挑选了一些我个人非常喜欢的模组，为游戏增添独特的玩法乐趣。

### 3. 🗑️ 清理过时内容
- 移除了所有未官方支持 1.21.1 的模组，保持整合包精简且无崩溃隐患。

### 4. 🔧 非官方重制与移植
- 加入了由社区制作的**非官方重制版**模组，让那些开发停滞的优秀模组得以继续使用（主要是Tetra）。

### 5. 🌌 末地玩法革新
- 用 **BetterEnd** 替换了原先不受支持的末地相关模组，将末地改造为一个令人叹为观止、内容丰富的最终领域。

Based on Create-Delight-Remake (1.20.1), this project supports Minecraft 1.21.1 Neoforge, combining updated existing mods, filling gaps with unofficial reworks, and removing unsupported mods to deliver a smooth and feature-rich experience. (Most importantly, it supports Aeronautics!!!)

---

## ✨ What Have I Done?

### 1. 🚀 Massive Mod Upgrades
- Updated the **vast majority** of mods to their latest 1.21.1-compatible versions, bringing better performance, stability, and new features.

### 2. 🧩 New Additions – My Favourites
- Added **Aeronautics** and several related popular mods to expand exploration and mechanical creativity.
- Carefully selected a few mods that I personally enjoy, adding unique gameplay twists.

### 3. 🗑️ Cleanup of Outdated Content
- Removed all mods that lack official 1.21.1 support, keeping the pack lean and crash-free.

### 4. 🔧 Unofficial Reworks & Backports
- Added **community-driven unofficial reworks** for mods that have stalled development, allowing excellent mods to continue being used (mainly Tetra).

### 5. 🌌 End-Game Redesign
- Replaced the old unsupported End-related mods with **BetterEnd**, transforming the End into a breathtaking, content-rich final frontier.
